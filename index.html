Welcome to Sri Maha Mariamman Temple

கோவாண்டி தமிழ் சங்கம் வழங்கும் அருள்மிகு ஸ்ரீ மகா மாரியம்மன் ஆலயம்.

Located in the heart of Govandi, Mumbai, **Sri Maha Mariamman Temple** stands as a magnificent spiritual beacon for the South Indian community and devotees across Maharashtra. Managed under the auspices of **Govandi Tamil Sangam (Reg. Plot No. 15, Shivaji Nagar)**, the temple preserves rich Dravidian architectural traditions and spiritual practices.

The temple features an exquisitely ornate multi-tiered **Rajagopuram** adorned with vibrant pastel sculptures depicting Goddess Mariamman in her divine forms, along with Lord Ganesha, Goddess Lakshmi, Goddess Saraswati, Goddess Durga, and celestial guardians.

Devotees gather daily for divine darshan, special *Abhishekam*, *Alangaram*, and traditional grand celebrations during *Thiruvizha* (Annual Temple Festival) and *Maha Kumbhabhishekam* occasions.

Presiding Deities & Iconography

Goddess Maha Mariamman

ஸ்ரீ மகா மாரியம்மன்

The supreme divine mother protecting devotees with health, prosperity, and rain. Seated in the sanctum sanctorum adorned with sacred garlands and golden ornaments.

Lord Ganesha (Vinayagar)

ஸ்ரீ கணபதி / விநாயகர்

Remover of all obstacles, enshrined at the entrance archway to bestow blessings before all auspicious beginnings and poojas.

Goddess Saraswati

ஸ்ரீ சரஸ்வதி அம்மன்

Goddess of wisdom, knowledge, and music with Veena sculpture on the Gopuram façade, blessing students and devotees.

Goddess Lakshmi

ஸ்ரீ லக்ஷ்மி அம்மன்

Goddess of wealth, prosperity, and auspiciousness depicted gracefully on lotus motifs within the temple architecture.

Goddess Durga / Mayuri

ஸ்ரீ துர்க்கை அம்மன்

Embodiement of courage and divine strength, depicted with sacred peacock vehicle (Mayur) on upper levels of the gopuram.

Guardian Parivara Devatas

பரிவார தெய்வங்கள்

Guardian deities and dwarapalakas meticulously sculpted to safeguard the sanctum and bless visitors.
